# Marketing & Creative UI Design Principles (`marketing.md`)

Use this guide when building or reviewing public-facing pages: landing pages, marketing sites, product announcements, creative artifacts, and conversion-oriented surfaces where the first impression matters most.

---

## 1. First Impression & Emotional Resonance

- **Impact with restraint:** Catching attention does not mean visual chaos or generic gradient blobs. Have a singular, distinct point of view.
- **Narrative flow:** A marketing page is a story told vertically. Every section must answer the visitor's subconscious question: *"Why should I care?"*, followed by *"Show me how it works"*, and finally *"How do I start?"*.
- **Domain-anchored aesthetic:** Draw visual inspiration directly from the industry, physical materials, and tools of the domain—not generic tech startup aesthetics.

---

## 2. Hero Section Architecture

- **Reject the defaults:**
  - ❌ Avoid: Centered headline + generic purple radial gradient + two buttons + standard floating 3D dashboard mockup.
  - ✅ Consider: Asymmetric layouts, interactive live previews, typography-led editorial layouts, or real product workflows front-and-center.
- **Clear Value Proposition:**
  - Primary headline should be bold, specific, and memorable.
  - Subhead should be concise (2-3 lines max) explaining the concrete mechanism or benefit.
  - Clear, primary Call-To-Action (CTA) with low friction.

---

## 3. Social Proof & Feature Storytelling

- **Feature Showcases:**
  - Show the actual product UI or tactile interactions, not generic stock illustrations.
  - Pair each capability with a concrete outcome, not just a technical spec.
- **Social Proof:**
  - Concrete numbers, recognizable logos, or authentic quotes.
  - Integrate social proof naturally near decision points (near CTAs or pricing tables).

---

## 4. Typography as Visual Identity

- **Headline Craft:** Choose display typefaces that have character and fit the product's spirit.
- **Scale Contrast:** Maintain dramatic contrast between hero typography and body copy.
- **Measure (Line Length):** Keep marketing copy narrow and readable (under 65 characters per line).

---

## 5. Micro-interactions & Motion

- **Subtle feedback:** Smooth transitions on hover and active states (150ms-250ms with ease-out curves).
- **Purposeful motion:** Motion should guide the eye or reveal context, not distract or cause motion sickness.
